Computer systems researcher · University of Virginia

Mohammad Sonji.

I am a Computer Science PhD candidate in UVA's Insight Lab, advised by Professor Adwait Jog. I study how modern computing systems use GPUs, memory, and interconnects—and how we can make their performance more predictable.

Previously, I completed an MS in Computer Science at the American University of Beirut with Professor Izzat El Hajj.

01 / Research

Systems, measured from the inside out.

My work connects architectural behavior with application-level outcomes, with an emphasis on GPU and high-performance systems.

01

GPU systems

Utilization, multi-tenancy, confidential computing, and the performance effects of memory and interconnect behavior.

02

Performance intelligence

Performance variability, execution modeling, and predictions that help systems make better runtime decisions.

03

Emerging architectures

Unified system memory, serverless GPU analytics, and hybrid compute-memory systems across new technology boundaries.
