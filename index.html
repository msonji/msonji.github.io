Computer Science PhD Candidate · University of Virginia

Mohammad Sonji

I am a Computer Science PhD candidate in UVA's Insight Lab, advised by Professor Adwait Jog. I study how modern computing systems use GPUs, memory, and interconnects, and how we can make their performance more predictable.

Previously, I completed an MS in Computer Science at the American University of Beirut with Professor Izzat El Hajj.
